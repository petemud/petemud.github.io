Went on to get a Bachelor's from [Admiral Makarov National University of Shipbuilding](https://iust.nuos.edu.ua/). Through competitive programming learned C++.
Participated in a couple of Huawei-organized competitions solving NP-complete problems ([1](https://algotester.com/gcc), [2](https://algotester.com/vmsc), [3](https://algotester.com/hot2021)). As a result, in December of 2021 went on a half-year business trip to Huawei in China as an intern, stayed and transferred to a full employee in Shanghai office at the end of August 2022.
I was always fascinated with provably-correct software, so I learned [Coq](https://coq.inria.fr) and started working on formal verification of [libvsync](https://github.com/open-s4c/libvsync) atomic primitives. Coq was rigorous, but quite labor intensive, so we switched to just as rigorous, but much faster [SMT-LIB](https://smt-lib.org) and [Boogie](https://boogie-docs.readthedocs.io). Have been here in Huawei since: doing formal verification of [HarmonyOS kernel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HongMeng_Kernel) - mostly in [Python](https://python.org).
